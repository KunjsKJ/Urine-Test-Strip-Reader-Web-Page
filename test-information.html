Urine Test Strip Diagnostic Parameters

A urine reagent strip contains several chemically treated pads. Each pad reacts with specific components present in the urine sample.

The reader system organizes these observations into a structured format for laboratory screening and academic demonstration.

Important Test Parameters

Parameter Purpose Unit Abbreviation
Glucose Indicates the presence of glucose in urine. mg/dL GLU
Protein Helps identify protein levels in urine. mg/dL PRO
Blood Detects blood-related indicators. Qualitative BLD
pH Indicates the acidity or alkalinity of urine. pH pH
Specific Gravity Provides information about urine concentration. Ratio SG

Diagnostic Process

The test strip is first immersed in the urine sample. After the required reaction time, the reagent pads are evaluated by the reader system.

Important parameters are highlighted during result interpretation, while abnormal observations can be reviewed separately.

Chemical measurements may be represented using mg/dL, while parameters such as pH are represented using their respective scales.

Common medical abbreviations include URO, RBC, WBC, BLD and SG.

Medical Disclaimer: This educational system is intended for laboratory demonstration and learning purposes. Test-strip readings should not be considered a standalone medical diagnosis.
